    Doubles method

    Pairs stay pairs.
    Maps need athletes.

    1. Freeze BWF’s 30 December 2025 lists.
    2. Keep 100 singles players and 50 pairs per doubles event.
    3. Assign half of each pair’s points to each partner.
    4. Deduplicate athletes across events.
