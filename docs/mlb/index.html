    Resolution method

    Official ID first.
    Geography second.

    1. Start with every player in official 2025 team hitting and pitching summaries.
    2. Keep exact team splits for traded players.
    3. Combine plate appearances and batters faced as cross-position workload.
    4. Accept coordinates only after country and state checks.
