    Cohort method

    On the field first.
    Geography second.

    1. Start with regular-season snap counts from nflverse.
    2. Keep anyone with at least one logged snap.
    3. Read birthplace fields from the ESPN athlete profile.
    4. Match city and verified country—plus US state—to GeoNames.
